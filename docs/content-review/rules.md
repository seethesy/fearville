THE BASICS

### How to play [anchor:how-to-play/1]

#### Two teams, one hidden Horror [anchor:how-to-play/2/0/0]

Good aims to kill the Horror. Evil usually wins when only two players remain alive. Character abilities can change these outcomes; the Storyteller resolves them.

#### Day and night [anchor:how-to-play/2/1/0]

By day, talk, share information, and bluff. At night, follow the Storyteller’s private instructions. Some characters act only on the first night or when a condition is met.

#### Nominations and execution [anchor:how-to-play/2/2/0]

Living players may nominate once each day; each player may be nominated once. Votes need at least half the living players, rounded up. The highest qualifying tally leads to execution; a tie means no execution.

#### Death is not the end [anchor:how-to-play/2/3/0]

Dead players still talk and win with their team. They cannot nominate and have one remaining vote. Abilities usually stop on death unless they say otherwise.

#### Information can be wrong [anchor:how-to-play/2/4/0]

Drunkenness and poison disable abilities without telling their owners. Information may be false. Ask the Storyteller privately when unsure.

#### Keep the game fair [anchor:how-to-play/2/5/0]

Bluffing is welcome; peeking at private screens is not. Respect the other players and ask the neutral Storyteller for help.

Quick reference. Character-specific rulings take precedence.

KEY TERMS

### Glossary [anchor:glossary-title]

Role descriptions use these words with a precise meaning.

Fear

A feared player’s ability malfunctions, exactly as if they were poisoned or drunk. They are not told. They still wake and make choices, but their ability has no effect and anything they learn may be false. Fear lasts only as long as the ability that caused it says.

Visit

When a player with a night ability chooses another player, they visit that player, unless the ability says otherwise. A feared player still visits. If a visit is redirected, it lands on the new player instead.

Save

A saved player cannot die that night. A save stops every attempt to kill that player that night, even several at once. Saves only work at night, and players can never save themselves.

Learn

The Storyteller privately tells you information that players cannot normally access. This is the main ability of information roles. If you are feared, poisoned or drunk, what you learn may be false.

Declare

An official public statement made during the day. If you are asked to declare at night, wait until morning to make your declaration. Example: “I am the Vigilante and I declare that I want to kill Player A.”

Neighbor

A player sitting directly next to you; everyone has two. Neighbors include both living and dead players unless an ability says otherwise, for example “living neighbors”.

AT THIS TABLE

### Using Fearville [anchor:using-fearville/1]

- **Join and prepare.** One Storyteller creates a room for 5–18 players. They assign roles randomly or manually, review setup, and start the game. Your character stays hidden until you reveal it on your own screen.

- **Follow Game flow.** Discovery, discussion, nominations, defense, and voting use shared timers. Check Messages at night and send choices privately to the Storyteller, who resolves abilities.

- **Vote openly.** Submit Yes or No in Game flow; everyone can see your answer. At this table, the nominee does not vote. Voting closes when all eligible players answer or the timer ends, whichever comes first. You can change your ballot until then. A counted Yes spends a dead player’s remaining vote; No does not. The Storyteller confirms the day’s outcome.

- **Keep your seat.** Closing a tab does not leave the game. Return from the home page in the same browser. Save your Sign in to your account to return from another device.

NIGHT RITE

### Puzzle guides [anchor:night-rite-title]

#### Escape Fearville [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.board/0]

Slide the wreckage aside and free the red carriage.

- [Diagram: A six by six board. The red carriage sits in the third row and must slide right through the gap in the fence. Arrows show pieces sliding only along their length.]

##### Free the red carriage [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.board/2/Free the red carriage/1]

Wreckage blocks the road out of Fearville. Your vigil wins by sliding the red carriage out through the gap in the fence. Every piece slides only along its length: side-to-side pieces never move up or down, and upright pieces never move sideways.

- [Diagram: Four players in a ring take turns in a random order. Each can make any number of slides, then passes. Turns repeat until the night or Rite ends.]

##### Keep sliding on your turn [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.board/2/Keep sliding on your turn/1]

Your vigil takes turns in a random order. Make as many legal slides as you want, then press Pass to hand over the board. Turns keep cycling until the Storyteller ends the night or closes the Rite.

- ##### Plan it by day [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.board/2/Plan it by day/1]

This escape takes about thirty slides. Between turns you see the board as you left it; the live board appears when your turn begins. By day, study the board together and agree on the next moves.

#### The Sphinx’s Riddles [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.grid/0]

Work out who was where, doing what, on the street.

- [Diagram: Five places along one street from west to east: Library, Garden, House, Market, Graveyard. Each has a spot for a person and an activity.]

##### Who, where and doing what? [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.grid/2/Who, where and doing what?/1]

Five people each spent the night at a different place on one street, each doing a different activity. Work out who was where and what they were doing. The places stand in a fixed order from west to east, exactly as the map shows them.

- [Diagram: Four clue cards: two open on the Rite’s first night, one on the second and one on the third.]

##### Your clue cards [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.grid/2/Your clue cards/1]

You hold four clue cards. Two are revealed on the Rite’s first night, then one more on each of the next two nights. No one else can see your cards, so read them aloud by day. The riddle needs everyone’s cards to solve.

- [Diagram: Examples on the street: next door means neighbouring places; two doors west means one place between them; one end of the street means the first or last place.]

##### Reading the clues [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.grid/2/Reading the clues/1]

“Next door” means neighbouring places. “Somewhere west of” means anywhere further west; “directly west of” means the very next place. “In some order” matches two groups without saying who goes with whom. “Either … or …, but not both” means exactly one part is true.

- ##### Answer at night [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.grid/2/Answer at night/1]

Arrange people and activities on your map whenever you like and save it. At night, submit your answer; you can replace it until the Storyteller ends the night or closes the Rite. The riddle is solved at dawn if more than half your vigil submitted the right answer. A card that clashes with the rest may be forged.

#### Ritual of Divination [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.ritual/0]

Three players, three rituals, three parts each.

- [Diagram: The omens holder reads symbols aloud, the grimoire keeper turns them into a setting, and the performer sets the altar.]

##### Three rituals, three parts [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.ritual/2/Three rituals, three parts/1]

Each of you performs your own ritual at an altar. For a second player’s ritual you hold the omens, and for the third player’s you keep the grimoire. Every ritual needs all three of you: omens read aloud, the grimoire turns them into settings, and the performer sets the altar.

- [Diagram: A grimoire entry turns a set of three symbols into one altar setting. Look-alike symbols such as a skull and a skull and crossbones never appear in the same set.]

##### Omens and the grimoire [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.ritual/2/Omens and the grimoire/1]

The omens are three sets of three symbols, one set per altar control, in no particular order. Find each set in the grimoire to learn which control it belongs to and how to set it. A set never holds two look-alike symbols, such as a skull and a skull and crossbones.

- ##### Perform the ritual [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.ritual/2/Perform the ritual/1]

From the night shown on your altar, you get one attempt per night. Set all three controls, then perform. A faltering ritual reveals nothing about what went wrong. The Divination is complete once two of the three rituals are performed. Omens can be misread, by accident or on purpose.

#### Alchemist’s Laboratory [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.lab/0]

Pour the brews until every vial holds one liquid.

- [Diagram: Two red layers on top of one vial pour onto a vial topped with red that has room for two.]

##### Sort every brew [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.lab/2/Sort every brew/1]

Ten liquids are mixed across twelve vials. Your vigil wins when every vial holds a single liquid. Tap a vial, then tap where to pour: the whole top layer pours onto the same liquid or into an empty vial, as far as there is room. A vial holds four.

- [Diagram: Four players in a ring take turns in a random order. Each can make any number of pours, then passes. Turns repeat until the night or Rite ends.]

##### Keep pouring on your turn [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.lab/2/Keep pouring on your turn/1]

Your vigil takes turns in a random order. Make as many legal pours as you want, then press Pass to hand over the vials. Turns keep cycling until the Storyteller ends the night or closes the Rite.

- ##### Undo, reset and ruin [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.lab/2/Undo, reset and ruin/1]

On your turn you may call a vote to undo tonight’s last pour or to reset to the very start; everyone else must agree. The vote stays open until everyone answers or the Rite closes. A pour that makes the brew impossible returns the vials to how they were at nightfall.

HORRORS OF FEARVILLE · 5–18 PLAYERS

### All 23 roles [anchor:hof-roles]

#### Fear, visits and saves [rules:horrors/6/HorrorsOfFearville/2/0/0]

Choosing a player at night visits them. A feared player’s ability malfunctions without them knowing. A saved player cannot die that night.

#### Declarations [rules:horrors/6/HorrorsOfFearville/2/1/0]

The Vigilante, the Researcher and a killed Hag act by declaring publicly during the day, from their Controls. The Storyteller rules on the outcome.

#### Travelers [rules:horrors/6/HorrorsOfFearville/2/2/0]

Executioner, Researcher and Guardian are neutral. They register as neither good nor evil and win in addition to whichever team wins. The Researcher learns their result at the end of the game.

#### How a death resolves [rules:horrors/6/HorrorsOfFearville/2/3/0]

A save or an impaired killer prevents a night death. The Guardian may die for their ward. After the Storyteller confirms a fatal night outcome, the Bodyguard learns the target’s name and may take their place. Death triggers then apply to whoever dies.

#### Villagers · Good [anchor:roles-horrors-townsfolk/0]

Abilities that help the town find evil.

##### Doctor [anchor:role-doctor/0]

Every night after the first, visit a player to save them.

##### Detective [anchor:role-detective/0]

Every night, visit two players to investigate them. You are told “Yes” if one of them is a Horror, or “No” if neither are. One random Villager secretly registers as a false positive, so they may produce a “Yes” despite being a Villager.

##### Watchman [anchor:role-watchman/0]

Every night after the first, visit a player to stay on lookout for them. You learn the names of all players who visit them.

##### Bodyguard [anchor:role-bodyguard/0]

After the Storyteller confirms that someone will die at night, learn their name and choose whether to die in their place.

##### Psychic [anchor:role-psychic/0]

Every night, the Storyteller chooses another player for you to visit. After the first night, that player’s role type will be different from the player you visited the previous night. You will not know their roles.

##### Judge [anchor:role-judge/0]

Every night, visit two players. You learn if they are the same alignment.

##### Mortician [anchor:role-mortician/0]

Once per game, at night, visit a dead player. Learn which roles have visited them in this game. If they were visited by 3 or more different players, learn 1 of their names.

##### Vigilante [anchor:role-vigilante/0]

Once per game, during the day, you may declare a player you want to kill. They may die instantly.

##### Sheriff [anchor:role-sheriff/0]

At the start of the game, learn that one of two players is a particular Haunt.

##### Librarian [anchor:role-hoflibrarian/0]

At the start of the game, learn that one of two players is a particular Villager.

##### Teacher [anchor:role-teacher/0]

At the start of the game, learn that one of your neighbors is a particular role.

##### Hunter [anchor:role-hunter/0]

If you are killed at night, a Haunt may die the same night.

#### Fools · Good [anchor:roles-horrors-outsider/0]

Good players whose abilities complicate the town’s work.

##### Drunk [anchor:role-drunk/0]

You do not know you are the Drunk. Instead, you are shown a random Villager role that you believe yourself to be. That role’s ability will not work as intended, but you will not know that.

##### Hag [anchor:role-hag/0]

If you are killed, declare another player to die. If they are Good, they may die that night.

##### Lost Wanderer [anchor:role-lostwanderer/0]

Every night, visit a player. Anyone else who visits them may be redirected to one of their neighbors.

#### Travelers · Neutral [anchor:roles-horrors-traveler/0]

Neither good nor evil. Each has a personal win condition, won alongside the team result.

##### Executioner [anchor:role-executioner/0]

At the start of the game, a random player becomes your execution target. You win if this player is executed. This player will never be the Horror.

##### Researcher [anchor:role-researcher/0]

Once per game, even while dead, you may declare the role of 3 living players. You win if you are correct about all of them.

##### Guardian [anchor:role-guardian/0]

At the start of the game, a random player becomes your ward. Any time this player is meant to die, you will die in their place. You win if your ward lives to the end of the game.

#### Haunts · Evil [anchor:roles-horrors-minion/0]

Support the Horror and mislead the town.

##### Banshee [anchor:role-banshee/0]

If you are executed, every Villager is feared that night.

##### Basilisk [anchor:role-basilisk/0]

Every night, visit one player to fear them that night.

##### Sphinx [anchor:role-sphinx/0]

Every night, receive a glimpse of every player’s role for the game.

#### Horrors · Evil [anchor:roles-horrors-demon/0]

The town’s hidden enemy.

##### Monster [anchor:role-monster/0]

Every night after the first, visit one player to kill them and choose one of their living neighbors. If the target dies, your chosen neighbor is feared the following night.

##### Wraith [anchor:role-wraith/0]

Every night after the first, visit one player to kill them that night. You may choose whether or not you are detected visiting this player.

HORRORS OF FEARVILLE

### Role tips [anchor:role-tips-title]

Ideas for playing each character. Your private Game flow shows only the tips for the character you believe you are.

#### Villagers [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/0]

##### Doctor [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/doctor/0]

- Protect players who can gather information across several nights.

- Keep your role private when possible; the Horror has reason to target you.

##### Detective [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/detective/0]

- Cross-check every result: one Villager is a false positive, and fear or drunkenness can distort your information.

- Share findings carefully so you can keep investigating.

##### Watchman [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/watchman/0]

- Watch players the Horror may target or players whose visitors you want to verify.

- A hidden Wraith may escape notice, so compare your results with other claims.

##### Bodyguard [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/bodyguard/0]

- Find an ally whose survival matters. If the Storyteller confirms they will die at night, you can take their place.

##### Psychic [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/psychic/0]

- Ask each player you visit what role type they claim, then look for a pattern across nights.

- A broken pattern can expose a false claim, but fear or drunkenness can mislead you.

##### Judge [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/judge/0]

- Compare players whose claims you can check against other information.

- Your answer reveals whether their alignments match, not what either role is.

##### Mortician [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/mortician/0]

- Wait for a dead player who attracted several visitors if you want the extra name clue.

- You can use this ability only once and only while alive.

##### Vigilante [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/vigilante/0]

- Use other players’ information before declaring a kill; a wrong target can hurt your team.

- A Haunt can be a useful target, especially one whose ability triggers on execution.

##### Sheriff [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/sheriff/0]

- Speak privately with the two named players before revealing the Haunt you were shown.

- Check their claims against other evidence; your first-night information may be unreliable.

- Completing the Night Rite can refresh your starting clue.

##### Librarian [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/hoflibrarian/0]

- Speak privately with the two named players before revealing the Villager you were shown.

- Check their claims against other evidence; your first-night information may be unreliable.

- Completing the Night Rite can refresh your starting clue.

##### Teacher [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/teacher/0]

- Ask your neighbors what they claim before revealing the role you were shown.

- Check their claims against other evidence; your first-night information may be unreliable.

- Completing the Night Rite can refresh your starting clue.

##### Hunter [anchor:role-tips/3/townsfolk/1/hunter/0]

- If the Horror kills you at night, a Haunt may die too.

- A convincing claim to be a valuable information role may draw the Horror’s attack.

#### Fools [anchor:role-tips/3/outsider/0]

##### Drunk [anchor:role-tips/3/outsider/1/drunk/0]

- You see guidance for the Villager you believe you are.

##### Hag [anchor:role-tips/3/outsider/1/hag/0]

- Your declaration after death can test a player’s claim to be Good.

- Coordinate with allies before choosing a target; a Good player may die.

##### Lost Wanderer [anchor:role-tips/3/outsider/1/lostwanderer/0]

- Visit an ally who may be targeted. Other visits to them may move to a neighbor.

- Coordinate with trusted players so your redirection protects more than it disrupts.

#### Travelers [anchor:role-tips/3/traveler/0]

##### Executioner [anchor:role-tips/3/traveler/1/executioner/0]

- Your target’s execution wins your personal goal even if you have died.

- You may bargain with either team, but reveal your target only when it helps.

##### Researcher [anchor:role-tips/3/traveler/1/researcher/0]

- Listen to claims and compare them with other players’ information before naming three roles.

- You may declare during the day even after death; all three guesses must be right.

##### Guardian [anchor:role-tips/3/traveler/1/guardian/0]

- Learn what your ward needs to survive; they may belong to any team.

- Your goal is for your ward to live to the end, even if you die first.

#### Haunts [anchor:role-tips/3/minion/0]

##### Banshee [anchor:role-tips/3/minion/1/banshee/0]

- You want to be executed. Death from a night attack or a Villager ability will not trigger your fear.

- Make a plausible claim that invites suspicion without making yourself an obvious kill target.

##### Basilisk [anchor:role-tips/3/minion/1/basilisk/0]

- Coordinate with the Horror to fear players whose abilities could expose or protect against them.

- Keeping an information role feared can be more useful than eliminating them immediately.

##### Sphinx [anchor:role-tips/3/minion/1/sphinx/0]

- Share useful glimpses with your evil team quickly, especially likely information and protection roles.

- Use your clues to help the Horror choose kills and the Basilisk choose fear targets.

#### Horrors [anchor:role-tips/3/demon/0]

##### Monster [anchor:role-tips/3/demon/1/monster/0]

- Choose a victim and one of their living neighbors to fear if that victim dies.

- Coordinate bluffs and target choices with your Haunts; a saved victim causes no fear.

##### Wraith [anchor:role-tips/3/demon/1/wraith/0]

- Choose whether your visit is visible to the Watchman and Mortician.

- Coordinate your target and cover story with your Haunts; a hidden visit can conceal your attack.

##### Reading the clues [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.grid/2/Reading the clues/1]

“Next door” means neighbouring places. “Somewhere west of” means anywhere further west; “directly west of” means the very next place. “In some order” matches two groups without saying who goes with whom. “Either … or …, but not both” means exactly one part is true.

- ##### Answer at night [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.grid/2/Answer at night/1]

Arrange people and activities on your map whenever you like and save it. At night, submit your answer; you can replace it until the Storyteller ends the night or closes the Rite. The riddle is solved at dawn if more than half your vigil submitted the right answer. A card that clashes with the rest may be forged.

#### Ritual of Divination [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.ritual/0]

Three players, three rituals, three parts each.

- [Diagram: The omens holder reads symbols aloud, the grimoire keeper turns them into a setting, and the performer sets the altar.]

##### Three rituals, three parts [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.ritual/2/Three rituals, three parts/1]

Each of you performs your own ritual at an altar. For a second player’s ritual you hold the omens, and for the third player’s you keep the grimoire. Every ritual needs all three of you: omens read aloud, the grimoire turns them into settings, and the performer sets the altar.

- [Diagram: A grimoire entry turns a set of three symbols into one altar setting. Look-alike symbols such as a skull and a skull and crossbones never appear in the same set.]

##### Omens and the grimoire [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.ritual/2/Omens and the grimoire/1]

The omens are three sets of three symbols, one set per altar control, in no particular order. Find each set in the grimoire to learn which control it belongs to and how to set it. A set never holds two look-alike symbols, such as a skull and a skull and crossbones.

- ##### Perform the ritual [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.ritual/2/Perform the ritual/1]

From the night shown on your altar, you get one attempt per night. Set all three controls, then perform. A faltering ritual reveals nothing about what went wrong. The Divination is complete once two of the three rituals are performed. Omens can be misread, by accident or on purpose.

#### Alchemist’s Laboratory [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.lab/0]

Pour the brews until every vial holds one liquid.

- [Diagram: Two red layers on top of one vial pour onto a vial topped with red that has room for two.]

##### Sort every brew [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.lab/2/Sort every brew/1]

Ten liquids are mixed across twelve vials. Your vigil wins when every vial holds a single liquid. Tap a vial, then tap where to pour: the whole top layer pours onto the same liquid or into an empty vial, as far as there is room. A vial holds four.

- [Diagram: Four players in a ring take turns in a random order. Each can make any number of pours, then passes. Turns repeat until the night or Rite ends.]

##### Keep pouring on your turn [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.lab/2/Keep pouring on your turn/1]

Your vigil takes turns in a random order. Make as many legal pours as you want, then press Pass to hand over the vials. Turns keep cycling until the Storyteller ends the night or closes the Rite.

- ##### Undo, reset and ruin [anchor:night-rite/2/RiteGuides/guide.lab/2/Undo, reset and ruin/1]

On your turn you may call a vote to undo tonight’s last pour or to reset to the very start; everyone else must agree. The vote stays open until everyone answers or the Rite closes. A pour that makes the brew impossible returns the vials to how they were at nightfall.

HOLY MOON AND STARS

### All 26 roles [anchor:hms-roles]

#### Four Horrors [rules:holyMoonStars/5/MoonAndStars/2/0/0]

The Grim Reaper marks and reaps, the Werewolf strikes three at a time on Nights 3, 6, 9…, the Dreameater guesses characters, and the Miasma spreads a plague. There is no Horror succession.

#### How a death resolves [rules:holyMoonStars/5/MoonAndStars/2/1/0]

Redirects first (Dazed, then the Nightmare), then prevention (Extra Life; the Celestial by day), then the Fateweaver may move the death once, and only then do death triggers happen (Constellation, Zealot, Priest, Moonshiner).

#### Visits [rules:holyMoonStars/5/MoonAndStars/2/2/0]

Choosing a player with a night ability visits them. The Spirit and Insomniac act after nearly every visit, and before the night’s Horror deaths resolve.

#### Dawn [rules:holyMoonStars/5/MoonAndStars/2/3/0]

Revivals from the Priest and Necromancer, the Gravedigger’s new character and the Priest’s self-revival happen at dawn. The Medium’s channel closes.

#### Villagers · Good [anchor:roles-holyMoonStars-townsfolk/0]

Abilities that help the town find evil.

##### Astrologist [anchor:role-astrologist/0]

At the start of the game, choose 3 players. You are told 3 roles: 1 belongs to one of them and 2 do not belong to anyone.

##### Mystic [anchor:role-mystic/0]

At the start of the game, choose 1 of your neighbors. You learn up to 3 other players they may be connected to.

##### Astronaut [anchor:role-astronaut/0]

At the start of the game, learn how far the nearest evil player is from you.

##### Stargazer [anchor:role-stargazer/0]

At the start of the game, choose a player. Alive or dead, you may ask the Storyteller who they spoke with or what they discussed. Answers are at Storyteller discretion.

##### Prophet [anchor:role-prophet/0]

At the start of the game, learn which Haunt and Horror characters are in play. If the Storyteller hears you reveal this, you and one of your neighbors may die that night.

##### Insomniac [anchor:role-insomniac/0]

Each night, learn which players visited you and how many of your neighbors visited another player.

##### Diviner [anchor:role-diviner/0]

Each night, ask the Storyteller a question. They answer with the name of a player you should speak to the next day.

##### Medium [anchor:role-medium/0]

Each night after the first, choose a dead player. They may use their night ability tonight (spent once-per-game abilities are not reset), and you share a private channel with them until dawn.

##### Spirit [anchor:role-spirit/0]

Each night, choose a player. Learn how many of their visitors are good and evil. If nobody visited, learn “0 people.” If you die, learn only the number of visitors.

##### Gravedigger [anchor:role-gravedigger/0]

Once per game, at night, choose a dead player. You may become their character and alignment at dawn. The Storyteller may refuse setup-changing characters.

##### Fateweaver [anchor:role-fateweaver/0]

The first time either of your living neighbors dies, you may redirect their death. The Storyteller may or may not follow your choice.

##### Priest [anchor:role-priest/0]

Once per game, at night, choose a dead player: they return at dawn (Villagers as themselves, Fools as themselves, Haunts as an out-of-play Villager). If you are killed, you return at the next dawn as an out-of-play Villager.

##### Atheist [anchor:role-atheist/0]

If the Atheist is in play, every player is good. Good wins by executing the Storyteller; evil wins when two players remain. The Storyteller may break any rule.

##### Celestial [anchor:role-celestial/0]

While you live, other good players cannot die during the day. If you are executed, everyone is poisoned until the next dusk and one Villager becomes drunk for the rest of the game. Evil learns whether a Celestial is in play.

#### Fools · Good [anchor:roles-holyMoonStars-outsider/0]

Good players whose abilities complicate the town’s work.

##### Constellation [anchor:role-constellation/0]

At the start of the game, choose 3 players to link. If one dies, the others may die too. Once per game, at night, guess all their characters: if correct, revive one or give one an extra life; if wrong, the link breaks and you and another member may die.

##### Lost Soul [anchor:role-lostsoul/0]

You secretly act as an out-of-play Villager, but you do not know which. Its ability does not work, and information is given only as unlabeled names or values.

##### Zealot [anchor:role-zealot/0]

When you die, the Storyteller gains a character ability from this script of their choice.

##### Dazed [anchor:role-dazed/0]

You are drunk and believe you are a Villager. Whenever another player targets you with an ability, the Storyteller may redirect it to one of your living neighbors or to a player of your choice.

#### Haunts · Evil [anchor:roles-holyMoonStars-minion/0]

Support the Horror and mislead the town.

##### Necromancer [anchor:role-necromancer/0]

Once per game, at night, choose a dead player: they return at dawn as evil (Haunts as themselves; others as an out-of-play Haunt). The Horror cannot be revived.

##### Moonshiner [anchor:role-moonshiner/0]

Adds a Fool to the game. Every Fool is drunk and believes they are a Villager, though their own Fool ability still works. After you die, the Fools may sober up and learn their real characters.

##### Nightmare [anchor:role-nightmare/0]

If you are executed, at night choose a player with a nighttime targeting ability. Once per game, when they choose a target, you have 60 seconds to redirect it without them knowing.

##### Astrophage [anchor:role-astrophage/0]

Once per game, during the day, you may end the day early. Not available with 4 or fewer living players.

#### Horrors · Evil [anchor:roles-holyMoonStars-demon/0]

The town’s hidden enemy.

##### Grim Reaper [anchor:role-grimreaper/0]

Each night, mark a player for death. At any time you may reap: every marked player dies.

##### Werewolf [anchor:role-werewolf/0]

Each night, prepare for the full moon. After two nights of preparation, choose 3 players to die (Nights 3, 6, 9…).

##### Dreameater [anchor:role-dreameater/0]

Each night, guess any number of players’ characters. Correct guesses die. At 3 total wrong guesses you die instead, and that night’s correct guesses do not count.

##### Miasma [anchor:role-miasma/0]

Each night, infect a player with the plague. The next night they die and both of their living neighbors are poisoned that night.
